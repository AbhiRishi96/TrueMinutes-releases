TrueMinutes Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

TrueMinutes is a local-first meeting notes app. Meeting recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes, and settings are stored on your Mac.

Google Drive sync

If you enable sync, TrueMinutes encrypts sync records locally before uploading them to Google Drive's private application data folder (appDataFolder). TrueMinutes cannot read arbitrary files in your Drive and does not share meeting content with other users.

Audio upload is off by default and requires a separate opt-in. Disconnecting Google Drive stops future uploads; existing local and remote data are not deleted automatically.

Data and security

OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. The vault key is generated locally and is required to decrypt synced content on another Mac. TrueMinutes does not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can disable sync or disconnect Google Drive in Settings. You can revoke TrueMinutes access from your Google Account security settings. For support, contact support@trueminutes.app.
