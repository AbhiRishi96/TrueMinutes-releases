TrueMinutes Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

TrueMinutes is a local-first meeting notes app. Meeting recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes, and settings are stored on your Mac.

Google Drive sync

If enabled, TrueMinutes encrypts sync records locally before uploading them to Google Drive's private application data folder (appDataFolder). TrueMinutes cannot read arbitrary Drive files and does not share meeting content with other users.

Audio upload is off by default and requires separate opt-in.

Security and choices

OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. The vault key is generated locally and is required to decrypt synced content on another Mac. You can disable or disconnect sync in Settings, or revoke access from your Google Account security settings.

For support, contact the TrueMinutes project maintainers through the GitHub issue tracker.
